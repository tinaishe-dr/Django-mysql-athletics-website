History


Our school Lowell and Finch Academy was founded in the year 1980 by the missionaries in London, UK. The school motto SAPIENTIA ET VERITAS, meaning wisdom and truth, has been the legacy among our students across generations. This website documents the athletic participation of the four houses of our school. Initially, our school had only two houses which are Lotus and Rose, when the population of the school was very less. With the growth of our school over the years and the growth of the city, we have four houses which are - Lotus, Rose, Lavender, and Daffodil. Lotus and Rose houses were founded in the year 1985, followed by the Lavender and Daffodil houses which were founded in the years 1900 and 1901 respectively. Our houses are identified with their unique colors which are Green for the Lotus house, Red for the Rose house, Blue for the Lavender house, and Gold for the Daffodil house. Each house is led by their house captains and vice captains. Our school hold votings to elect the captains and vice captains who serve as house leaders for a year. We aim to teach responsibility, discipline, and leadership skills to our students. We firmly believe that athletic skills are a strong foundations that would build our students' inner strength and would prepare them to thrive in the real world as they move forward in their future.
